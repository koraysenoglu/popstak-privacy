# Privacy Policy for Popstak: Block Puzzle

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

Popstak: Block Puzzle ("Popstak", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Popstak: Block Puzzle.

## Information We Collect

Popstak does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying personal information.

The app stores certain gameplay and preference data locally on your device, such as game progress, high score, language settings, sound preferences, and tutorial completion status.

## Advertising

Popstak uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including:

- banner advertisements during gameplay
- rewarded advertisements that may be voluntarily watched to continue a game

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process certain information for advertising, fraud prevention, analytics, diagnostics, and service improvement purposes.

Depending on your device, region, privacy settings, and consent choices, this information may include:

- IP address
- approximate location derived from the IP address
- device identifiers
- advertising-related information
- information about advertisements shown or interacted with
- app interaction information
- performance and diagnostic information

Google may process this information in accordance with its own privacy policies and advertising policies.

You can learn more about how Google handles data in its advertising products at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Popstak may display a consent message before requesting certain advertising-related data.

Users may be able to manage or withdraw their advertising privacy choices through the consent options provided in the app.

Advertising behavior may vary depending on your consent choices, device settings, and region.

## App Tracking Transparency

Popstak does not currently request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

If tracking-related functionality is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## Third-Party Services

Popstak currently uses:

- Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

These third-party services may process information independently under their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

Popstak is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly ask users to provide directly identifying personal information.

If our data practices change in a way that affects children or requires additional parental consent, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Gameplay and preference information stored locally by Popstak remains on your device unless it is removed by the app, replaced by newer data, or deleted when the app is removed.

Data processed by Google through AdMob is subject to Google's own retention practices and policies.

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to minimize the amount of personal information processed directly by Popstak.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Popstak's features, advertising systems, third-party services, or legal requirements change.
